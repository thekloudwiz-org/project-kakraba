## Getting Started

### What is the Creator Portal?

The Creator Portal is your command center for:
- Uploading and managing digital content
- Creating products and setting prices
- Tracking revenue and analytics
- Managing your creator profile
- Engaging with your fan base

### Accessing the Portal

1. Visit `https://kakraba.thekloudwiz.com`
2. Click **"I'm a Creator"**
3. You'll be redirected to `https://kakraba.thekloudwiz.com/creator`

## Account Setup

### Registration

1. **Navigate to Registration:**
   - Click "Sign Up" on the Creator Portal
   
2. **Fill in Your Details:**
   - **Email:** Your professional email address
   - **Password:** Strong password (min 8 characters, uppercase, lowercase, number, special character)
   - **Display Name:** Your creator name (visible to fans)
   - **Bio:** Brief description of your content (optional)

3. **Verify Your Email:**
   - Check your inbox for verification email
   - Click the verification link
   - Return to login page

4. **Complete Your Profile:**
   - Upload profile picture
   - Add detailed bio
   - Set up payment information

### First Login

After verification:
1. Enter your email and password
2. Click "Sign In"
3. You'll be taken to your Dashboard

## Content Management

### Uploading Content

#### Supported File Types

- **Video:** MP4, MOV, AVI (max 5GB)
- **Audio:** MP3, WAV, FLAC (max 500MB)
- **PDF:** Documents, ebooks (max 100MB)
- **Images:** JPG, PNG, GIF (max 50MB)

#### Upload Process

1. **Navigate to Upload:**
   - Click "Content" in sidebar
   - Click "Upload New Content"

2. **Select Your File:**
   - Drag and drop file
   - Or click "Browse" to select

3. **Add Metadata:**
   - **Title:** Descriptive title for your content
   - **Description:** Detailed description (supports markdown)
   - **Category:** Select appropriate category
   - **Tags:** Add relevant tags for discovery

4. **Upload:**
   - Click "Upload"
   - Wait for progress bar to complete
   - You'll see confirmation when done

#### Upload Tips

- **Use descriptive titles:** Help fans find your content
- **Write detailed descriptions:** Explain what fans will get
- **Add high-quality thumbnails:** First impressions matter
- **Tag appropriately:** Improve discoverability
- **Check file quality:** Ensure good audio/video quality before uploading

### Managing Your Content Library

#### Viewing Content

1. Navigate to "Content" in sidebar
2. View all your uploaded content
3. Filter by:
   - Content type (video, audio, PDF, image)
   - Upload date
   - Status (published, draft)

#### Editing Content

1. Click on content item
2. Click "Edit"
3. Update:
   - Title
   - Description
   - Thumbnail
   - Tags
4. Click "Save Changes"

#### Deleting Content

⚠️ **Warning:** Deletion is permanent!

1. Click on content item
2. Click "Delete"
3. Confirm deletion
4. Content will be removed from all products

**Note:** If content is part of active products, you'll be warned before deletion.

### Content Organization

#### Best Practices

- **Create series:** Group related content together
- **Use consistent naming:** Make content easy to find
- **Regular uploads:** Keep fans engaged
- **Quality over quantity:** Focus on valuable content

## Product Creation

### What is a Product?

A product is how you package and sell your content:
- **Single Item:** One piece of content
- **Bundle:** Multiple pieces of content together
- **Subscription Access:** Recurring access to your library

### Creating a Product

1. **Navigate to Products:**
   - Click "Products" in sidebar
   - Click "Create New Product"

2. **Select Content:**
   - Choose content items to include
   - Single item or multiple (bundle)

3. **Set Pricing:**
   - **Price:** Amount in USD (min $0.99, max $999.99)
   - **Access Type:**
     - **Purchase:** One-time payment, permanent access
     - **Rental:** Time-limited access
   
4. **Configure Access:**
   - **Download Quota:** Number of downloads allowed (0 = unlimited)
   - **Streaming:** Always available
   - **Subscription Eligible:** Include in subscription access

5. **Add Product Details:**
   - **Title:** Product name
   - **Description:** What fans get
   - **Preview Image:** Attractive product image
   - **Preview Content:** Sample for fans to preview

6. **Publish:**
   - Click "Create Product"
   - Product is immediately available to fans

### Product Types

#### Single Content Product

**Best for:**
- Individual videos
- Single audio tracks
- Standalone PDFs
- One-off content

**Example:**
```
Title: "Advanced Photography Tutorial"
Price: $9.99
Content: 1 video (45 minutes)
Downloads: 3
```

#### Bundle Product

**Best for:**
- Course materials
- Album releases
- Content series
- Complete collections

**Example:**
```
Title: "Complete Photography Course"
Price: $49.99
Content: 10 videos + 5 PDFs
Downloads: Unlimited
```

#### Subscription Product

**Best for:**
- Regular content creators
- Ongoing series
- Community access
- Exclusive content

**Example:**
```
Title: "Monthly Photography Masterclass"
Price: $19.99/month
Content: All new content + archive
Downloads: Unlimited
```

### Managing Products

#### Editing Products

1. Navigate to "Products"
2. Click on product
3. Click "Edit"
4. Update details
5. Save changes

**Note:** Price changes don't affect existing purchases.

#### Deactivating Products

1. Click on product
2. Toggle "Active" switch
3. Product hidden from fans
4. Existing purchases still work

#### Product Analytics

View for each product:
- Total sales
- Revenue generated
- Views and conversions
- Popular content items

## Troubleshooting

### Upload Issues

#### Upload Fails

**Problem:** Upload doesn't complete

**Solutions:**
1. Check file size limits
2. Verify file format
3. Check internet connection
4. Try different browser
5. Clear browser cache

#### Slow Upload

**Problem:** Upload takes too long

**Solutions:**
1. Check internet speed
2. Upload during off-peak hours
3. Compress large files
4. Use wired connection

### Product Issues

#### Product Not Visible

**Problem:** Fans can't see product

**Solutions:**
1. Check product is "Active"
2. Verify content is published
3. Ensure price is set
4. Check product has description

#### Can't Edit Product

**Problem:** Edit button not working

**Solutions:**
1. Refresh page
2. Check you're the owner
3. Clear browser cache
4. Try different browser

### Payment Issues

#### Payout Delayed

**Problem:** Payment not received

**Solutions:**
1. Check payout schedule
2. Verify minimum threshold met
3. Confirm bank details correct
4. Check Stripe account status
5. Contact support

#### Revenue Mismatch

**Problem:** Revenue doesn't match expectations

**Solutions:**
1. Check date range
2. Account for platform fees
3. Verify all transactions
4. Export data for review

### Account Issues

#### Can't Login

**Problem:** Login fails

**Solutions:**
1. Verify email address
2. Reset password
3. Check email verification
4. Clear browser cookies
5. Try incognito mode

#### Profile Not Updating

**Problem:** Changes don't save

**Solutions:**
1. Check all required fields
2. Verify image file size
3. Check internet connection
4. Try again later
