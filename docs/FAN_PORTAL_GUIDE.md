## Getting Started

### What is the Fan Portal?

The Fan Portal is your gateway to:
- Discover amazing creator content
- Purchase individual items or bundles
- Subscribe to your favorite creators
- Stream or download content
- Manage your library

### Accessing the Portal

1. Visit `https://kakraba.thekloudwiz.com`
2. Click **"I'm a Fan"**
3. You'll be redirected to `https://kakraba.thekloudwiz.com/fan`

## Account Setup

### Registration

1. **Navigate to Registration:**
   - Click "Sign Up" on the Fan Portal
   
2. **Fill in Your Details:**
   - **Email:** Your email address
   - **Password:** Strong password (min 8 characters)
   - **Display Name:** Your username (visible to creators)

3. **Verify Your Email:**
   - Check your inbox for verification email
   - Click the verification link
   - Return to login page

4. **Complete Your Profile:**
   - Upload profile picture (optional)
   - Set notification preferences

### First Login

After verification:
1. Enter your email and password
2. Click "Sign In"
3. You'll be taken to the Discovery page

## Making Purchases

### One-Time Purchase

#### Purchase Process

1. **Select Product:**
   - Browse or search
   - Click on product

2. **Review Details:**
   - Check what's included
   - Read description
   - View preview

3. **Add to Cart:**
   - Click "Buy Now"
   - Or "Add to Cart" for multiple items

4. **Checkout:**
   - Review cart
   - Enter payment details
   - Click "Complete Purchase"

5. **Confirmation:**
   - Receive email confirmation
   - Content added to your library

#### Payment Methods

Accepted:
- Credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex)
- Debit cards
- Apple Pay
- Google Pay

**Security:**
- All payments processed by Stripe
- PCI DSS compliant
- Your card details never stored on our servers

### Subscriptions

3. **Subscribe:**

4. **Access Content:**

#### Subscription Benefits

## Accessing Content

### Your Library

#### Filtering Library

### Streaming Content

#### Video/Audio Streaming

1. **Select Content:**
   - Click on video or audio in library

2. **Player Opens:**
   - Content loads automatically
   - Controls available:
     - Play/Pause
     - Volume
     - Seek
     - Fullscreen (video)
     - Playback speed

3. **Stream Quality:**
   - Automatically adjusts to connection
   - Manual quality selection available

#### Streaming Tips

- **Stable Connection:** Use WiFi for best quality
- **Save Data:** Lower quality on mobile
- **Offline:** Download for offline viewing

### Downloading Content

#### Download Process

1. **Select Content:**
   - Click on content in library
   - Click "Download" button

2. **Check Quota:**
   - View remaining downloads
   - Some content has download limits

3. **Download:**
   - File downloads to your device
   - Original quality

4. **Access Offline:**
   - Open downloaded file anytime
   - No internet required

#### Download Limits

- **Purchased Content:** May have download quota (e.g., 5 downloads)
- **Subscription Content:** Usually unlimited
- **Streaming:** Never counts against quota

**Example:**
```
Product: Photography Tutorial
Download Quota: 3 downloads
Downloads Used: 1
Remaining: 2
Streaming: Unlimited
```

### Content Formats

#### Video Content

- **Formats:** MP4
- **Quality:** Up to 4K
- **Streaming:** Adaptive bitrate
- **Download:** Original quality

#### Audio Content

- **Formats:** MP3, FLAC
- **Quality:** Up to 320kbps
- **Streaming:** Available
- **Download:** Original quality

#### PDF Content

- **Viewing:** In-browser viewer
- **Download:** Original PDF
- **Printing:** Allowed (unless restricted)

#### Image Content

- **Viewing:** Gallery view
- **Download:** High resolution
- **Formats:** JPG, PNG

## Managing Subscriptions

### Viewing Subscriptions

1. Navigate to "Subscriptions"
2. View all active subscriptions:
   - Creator name
   - Monthly price
   - Next billing date
   - Status

### Subscription Details

For each subscription:
- **Start Date:** When you subscribed
- **Billing Cycle:** Monthly
- **Next Payment:** Date and amount
- **Payment Method:** Card on file
- **Content Access:** What's included

### Updating Payment Method

1. Go to "Subscriptions"
2. Click on subscription
3. Click "Update Payment Method"
4. Enter new card details
5. Save changes

**Note:** Changes apply to next billing cycle.

### Canceling Subscriptions

#### Cancellation Process

1. **Navigate to Subscriptions:**
   - Click "Subscriptions"
   - Select subscription to cancel

2. **Cancel:**
   - Click "Cancel Subscription"
   - Confirm cancellation

3. **Access Retained:**
   - Keep access until end of billing period
   - No refund for current period
   - No future charges

#### Reactivating Subscriptions

1. Visit creator profile
2. Click "Resubscribe"
3. Confirm reactivation
4. Immediate access restored

### Subscription Billing

#### Billing Cycle

- **Frequency:** Monthly
- **Billing Date:** Same day each month
- **Automatic:** Charged automatically
- **Notification:** Email before each charge

#### Failed Payments

If payment fails:
1. Email notification sent
2. Retry after 3 days
3. Retry after 7 days
4. Subscription canceled if still failing
5. Access removed

**To fix:**
- Update payment method
- Ensure sufficient funds
- Contact support if issues persist

## Troubleshooting

### Purchase Issues

#### Payment Declined

**Problem:** Card payment fails

**Solutions:**
1. Check card details correct
2. Verify sufficient funds
3. Contact your bank
4. Try different card
5. Check billing address matches

#### Purchase Not in Library

**Problem:** Bought content not showing

**Solutions:**
1. Refresh library page
2. Check email confirmation
3. Verify payment completed
4. Wait 5 minutes and check again
5. Contact support with order number

### Streaming Issues

#### Video Won't Play

**Problem:** Video doesn't load

**Solutions:**
1. Check internet connection
2. Refresh page
3. Try different browser
4. Clear browser cache
5. Disable browser extensions
6. Check if content still available

#### Buffering/Lag

**Problem:** Video keeps buffering

**Solutions:**
1. Check internet speed (need 5+ Mbps)
2. Lower video quality
3. Pause and let buffer
4. Close other tabs/apps
5. Use wired connection
6. Try different time (less congestion)

#### Audio Out of Sync

**Problem:** Audio doesn't match video

**Solutions:**
1. Refresh page
2. Try different browser
3. Check browser is updated
4. Report issue to support

### Download Issues

#### Download Fails

**Problem:** Download doesn't complete

**Solutions:**
1. Check internet connection
2. Verify download quota remaining
3. Check storage space on device
4. Try different browser
5. Disable download managers

#### Can't Find Downloaded File

**Problem:** Downloaded file missing

**Solutions:**
1. Check Downloads folder
2. Check browser download history
3. Search computer for filename
4. Re-download if needed

#### Download Quota Exhausted

**Problem:** No downloads remaining

**Solutions:**
1. Stream content instead (unlimited)
2. Contact creator for additional downloads
3. Purchase again if needed
4. Subscribe for unlimited access

### Subscription Issues

#### Charged Twice

**Problem:** Double billing

**Solutions:**
1. Check bank statement carefully
2. Verify only one active subscription
3. Check for pending vs completed charges
4. Contact support with transaction IDs

#### Can't Cancel Subscription

**Problem:** Cancel button not working

**Solutions:**
1. Refresh page
2. Try different browser
3. Clear browser cache
4. Contact support for manual cancellation

#### Lost Access After Canceling

**Problem:** Access removed immediately

**Solutions:**
1. Check cancellation date
2. Verify billing period ended
3. Contact support if access should remain
4. Resubscribe if needed

### Account Issues

#### Can't Login

**Problem:** Login fails

**Solutions:**
1. Verify email address
2. Reset password
3. Check email verification
4. Clear browser cookies
5. Try incognito mode

#### Forgot Password

**Problem:** Can't remember password

**Solutions:**
1. Click "Forgot Password"
2. Enter email address
3. Check email for reset link
4. Create new password
5. Login with new password

#### Account Locked

**Problem:** Too many failed login attempts

**Solutions:**
1. Wait 30 minutes
2. Reset password
3. Contact support if still locked
